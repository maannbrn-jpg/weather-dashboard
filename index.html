Future IT Careers

There is a place in technology for different interests, strengths, and ways of thinking.

</>

Software Developer

Creates programs and apps that help people and businesses.

Skills: Programming, logic, and problem solving.

Why it matters: Software powers many of the tools we use every day.

</>

Web Developer

Builds and maintains websites that are useful and easy to use.

Skills: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and creativity.

Why it matters: Organizations need a helpful online presence.

🔒

Cybersecurity Specialist

Protects computers, networks, and information from digital threats.

Skills: Attention to detail, analysis, and ethics.

Why it matters: More connected systems need stronger protection.

📊

Data Scientist

Studies data to discover patterns and support better decisions.

Skills: Statistics, research, and critical thinking.

Why it matters: Good data can improve health, business, and communities.

AI

AI Engineer

Develops systems that can learn, recognize patterns, or assist people.

Skills: Math, programming, and curiosity.

Why it matters: Responsible AI can help solve complex problems.

☁

Cloud Engineer

Manages online systems where applications and data are stored.

Skills: Networking, organization, and troubleshooting.

Why it matters: Cloud services let people work and collaborate anywhere.

🎮

Game Developer

Combines code, art, and storytelling to make interactive games.

Skills: Programming, creativity, and teamwork.

Why it matters: Games are also used for learning, training, and simulation.

🖥

Network Administrator

Keeps an organization’s computers and connections working reliably.

Skills: Networking, communication, and patience.

Why it matters: Reliable networks keep people and services connected.
